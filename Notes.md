90–100%: rock-solid: phone_visible (100), talking (96), occluded (96), hand_state (92). 

These are objective and prompt-independent — trust them.

50–75%: usable but noisier. posture (71), laptop_visible (62), activity (58), engagement_level (58). 

Wording shifts the answer sometimes; fine to keep, but expect some label noise.

<50%: fragile. gaze_direction (50), attention_target (46). 

The variants disagree about half the time — these categories are genuinely ambiguous or overlapping. attention_target in particular overlaps with gaze_direction and engagement_level, so it may be partly redundant.
